What this model can claim (Ezekiel 40–47)

1. Explicitly Measured in Ezekiel 40–47

  • Long Cubit (Ezek 40:5; 43:13): each cubit equals one ordinary cubit plus a handbreadth (modeled at 0.525 m by default; adjustable via the Measures slider).
  • Outer Wall & Six Gatehouses: surrounding wall 6 × 6 cubits; East, North & South outer/inner gates 50 × 25 cubits with guardrooms & palm pilasters (Ezek 40).
  • Courts, Lower Pavement & Kitchens: 100-cubit outer court span, lower pavement with 30 chambers, 4 corner boiling kitchens 40 × 30 cubits (Ezek 46:21–24).
  • Stepped Altar (Ezek 43:13–17): stepped base, 16c lower settle, 14c upper settle, 12 × 12c Ariel hearth, 4 horns, steps facing east.
  • Temple House, Side Chambers & Binyan (Ezek 41–42).

2. Textual Variants & Representational Notes

  • 500 Cubits (LXX) vs. 500 Reeds (MT) in Ezek 42:16–20: modeled as 500 × 500 cubits so all internal Ezekiel 40–42 spans sum identically to 500 cubits.
  • Porch Depth (Ezek 40:49): MT reads 11 cubits; LXX reads 12 cubits (modeled at 12 cubits).
  • Aniconic Glory & Living River (Ezek 43:1–5; 47:1–12): rendered purely with golden radiant light beams and crystal-teal flowing water.
