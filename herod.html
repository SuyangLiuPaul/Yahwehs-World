What this reconstruction can claim (Herod’s Temple)

1. Attested in the Gospels, Acts & Epistles

  • Monumental Stones & 46 Years of Building (Mark 13:1–2; John 2:20).
  • Outer Courts, Solomon’s Portico, Soreg & Beautiful Gate (John 10:23; Acts 3:2; 21:28).
  • Treasury, Sanctuary Vessels, Veil & Antonia Steps (Mark 12:41; Luke 1:9; Acts 21:40).

2. Eyewitness Descriptions in Flavius Josephus (War 5.5 · Ant. 15.11)

  • Royal Stoa of 162 Corinthian columns & southern gates (Josephus, Ant. 15.411–416).
  • Soreg inscriptions, Golden Vine, Veil & Antonia keep (Josephus, War 5.193–245).

3. Rabbinic Dimensions in Mishnah Middot (Yoma · Sukkah · Shekalim)

  • 135 × 135c Court of the Women, 13 chests, 4 Sukkot lampstands & 15 semicircular steps (Middot 2:1–5).
  • 187 × 135c Azarah, 32 × 32c Altar & 100 × 100 × 100c T-plan Sanctuary (Middot 3–5).

4. Source Divergences & Strict Aniconic Fidelity

  • Source divergences disclosed transparently (War 5 vs. Middot 2–3).
  • Empty Second-Temple Most Holy Place & bare Foundation Stone (War 5.219; Yoma 5:2).
