Solomon's Royal Precinct

Step inside the Great Court of three stone courses and cedar beams: enter the 100×50×30-cubit House of the Forest of Lebanon with its cedar colonnade, upper gallery, three ranks of opposing windows and 500 beaten-gold shields; cross the 50×30 Porch of Pillars into the cedar-paneled Hall of Judgment before the six-stepped Ivory-and-Gold Lion Throne; and look north toward Yahweh's Temple.

What this model can claim (1 Kings 7 & 10)

1. Explicitly Measured & Counted in 1 Kings 7 & 10 (2 Chronicles 9)

  • House of the Forest of Lebanon (1 Kgs 7:2–5): 100 × 50 × 30 cubits, 45 cedar chamber-beams (15 per row), and 3 tiers of opposing squared windows.
  • 500 Beaten-Gold Shields & Pure-Gold Vessels (1 Kgs 10:16–21): 200 large gold shields, 300 gold bucklers, and pure-gold drinking vessels.
  • Porch of Pillars (1 Kgs 7:6): 50 × 30 cubits with fore-porch and threshold canopy (av).
  • Great Ivory Throne Overlaid with Pure Gold (1 Kgs 10:18–20; 2 Chr 9:18): 6 steps, golden footstool, rounded back, 2 armrest lions, and 12 step lions.
  • Foundations & Great Court Masonry (1 Kgs 7:9–12): 8- and 10-cubit foundation blocks; 3 ashlar courses + 1 cedar beam course.

2. Textual Variant: 4 Rows (MT) vs. 3 Rows (LXX) in 1 Kings 7:2–3

  • MT 4 rows vs. LXX 3 rows (1 Kgs 7:2–3): modeled with 4 rows × 15 pillars carrying 3 × 15 (= 45) side chambers, with both 3-row and 4-row variants inspectable in Measures.

3. Unmeasured in Scripture & Labelled Display Choices

  • Hall of the Throne & Inner Residence (1 Kgs 7:7–8): cubit lengths and widths are unstated in the text and are labelled display choices.
  • Great Court Extent, Gallery Stair & Northern Temple Vista (1 Kgs 7:12; 10:5): architectural framing choices, clearly distinguished from stated dimensions.
