What this model can claim (Revelation 21:9–22:5)

1. Explicitly Measured & Named in Revelation 21–22

  • Macro Cube of 12,000 Stadia (Rev 21:16): length, width, and height equal 12,000 stadia (~2,220 km).
  • 144-Cubit Crystalline Jasper Wall (Rev 21:17–18): ~64.08 m at 0.445 m/cubit, jasper clear as crystal.
  • Twelve Single-Pearl Gates (Rev 21:12–13, 21, 25): three per side, each gate one pearl, permanently open.
  • Twelve Named Gemstone Foundations (Rev 21:19–20): Jasper through Amethyst in exact sequence.
  • Golden-Glass Street, Crystal River & Trees of Life (Rev 22:1–2).

2. Unstated Architectural Details & Display Choices

  • 144 Cubits as Wall Height vs. Thickness: modeled at 1:1 scale as a 144-cubit walkable jasper wall height.
  • Symbolic Apostolic Stelae: marked I–XII with each named mineral rather than unstated individual name pairings.
  • Boulevard Span & Rising Horizon: classical faceted gold-glass colonnades and stepped terraces rather than modern towers.
  • Strictly Aniconic Sanctuary & No Temple (Rev 21:22–23; 22:5): pure jasper-and-carnelian light, emerald rainbow, and crystal springhead with zero bodily figures.
