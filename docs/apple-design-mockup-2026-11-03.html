Yahweh's World - motion and phone-nav mock-up

For owner approval. Nothing on this page is in the app. It shows three feel changes from the Apple motion audit (docs/apple-design-audit-2026-11-03.md) that change how the product looks and behaves, so they need a yes before anyone builds them. Try each on a touch device if you can. Honours your system's reduced-motion setting.

1 - Globe: what happens when you let go

Today the globe stops the instant your finger lifts (decided in BATCH-5 because the old damping left a 213-250 px tail). Apple's behaviour is the opposite: the globe keeps the speed your finger had and slows the way a scroll view does. Drag the globe below, flick it, and compare.

Glide: grab the globe while it is travelling and it follows your finger from where it is, carrying its speed - nothing finishes first. Zoom buttons: or scroll / pinch over the globe.

  • What it costs: a flick can carry the globe past the place you aimed at. Apple accepts that because a drag is exploring; a tap on a place is unaffected.
  • Reduced motion: coast and glide turn off automatically (cut, stop); zoom becomes a fast fade of the step.
  • Risk: it reverses a recorded decision (BATCH-5), and the on-screen label scale in markers.ts assumes the camera is still when it is read.

2 - Panels as sheets

Today the place panel, event card, route card and menus appear and disappear with no motion at all (10 of 10 surfaces measured: transition 0 s, no animation). Below, the same card as a sheet: it rises from where it was opened, follows the finger one-to-one, snaps to the nearest of three heights using the throw (projected landing, not the release point), and resists with rubber-banding at the top.

Jerusalem

Place - 2 Samuel 5 - Psalm 122

David took the stronghold of Zion and it became the City of David. Drag this sheet by anywhere on its face: up for more, down to put it away.

The middle height shows the card without hiding the map; the full height is for reading.

Behaviour
Open
  • Spring: response 0.30 s, damping 0.8 when thrown, 1.0 when tapped to open (Apple's drawer values).
  • Velocity handoff: the spring starts at the finger's release speed, so there is no seam between drag and settle.
  • Symmetry: it leaves the way it came (down), and a grab mid-close reverses it from where it is.
  • Scrim: only at the full height, so the map stays usable at the middle height.
  • Alternative to a gesture: the existing close button stays; Escape closes.

3 - Phone navigation

At 375 px the top bar spends its width on the brand, 全部, 路线 and the language switch, and leaves the tab strip 28-34 px wide: one tab, cut in half ("Glo"). The current page is off the edge on four of five pages (fixed so it scrolls into view; this is the structural fix). Apple's answer for top-level destinations on a phone is a bottom tab bar (tab-bars.md: a tab bar for navigation, tabs never hidden).
